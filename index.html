About Us

A Trusted Name in
Medical Devices Manufacturing

Aayan Cotton Industries is a renowned manufacturer and one of the largest exporters of premium-quality medical devices in Pakistan. Established in 2000 at Depalpur Road, Okara, Punjab, Pakistan.

We have earned a reputation for excellence in the healthcare manufacturing sector, expanding our product range to encompass an extensive variety of medical and surgical products.

  • Orthopedic Under-Cast Padding
  • Zig-Zag Cotton
  • Maternity & Sanitary Napkins
  • Bleached Cotton
  • Gauze Bandages & Rolls
  • Elastic Crepe Bandages
  • Sterile Gauze Swabs
  • Surgical Gowns

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

✔️

Our Vision

To lead the industry as a trusted manufacturer of Absorbent Cotton Wool, Surgical Dressings, and other healthcare products that enhance the quality of life for our customers. We aspire to set new benchmarks in product excellence, innovation, and reliability while continuously adding value to the lives of those who rely on our brand. By prioritizing quality and customer satisfaction, we aim to be a global leader in the medical manufacturing sector. ACI is committed to playing an active and impactful role in the growth and development of the worldwide healthcare community, fostering progress through integrity, dedication, and excellence.

💚

Our Mission

to uphold an unwavering commitment to delivering premium-quality medical products that prioritize safety, comfort, and reliability. Since our establishment, we have remained steadfast in our promise to never compromise on quality, ensuring that every product meets the highest standards of excellence. We strive to enhance the experience of healthcare professionals and patients alike by providing innovative, comfortable, and effective solutions. With a focus on infection prevention and efficiency, we aim to support our partners with professional services that add value to their operations. At ACI, we are dedicated to advancing healthcare through trust, integrity, and quality-driven manufacturing.

From Fibre to Finish
01

Raw Cotton

Hand-selected natural cotton fibre arrives from the finest farms, the starting point of every product.

02

Selection & Purification

Meticulously selected premium fibers are deeply cleaned and purified for medical-grade absorbency and signature softness.

03

Precision Processing

Cleaned fibers undergo automated processing to form flawlessly structured products engineered for clinical safety.

04

Quality & Packaging

Every batch is tested and sealed under ISO 9001 & 13485 standards, ready for the world.

05

Global Delivery

Finished medical cotton ships to 20+ countries, trusted by hospitals and distributors worldwide.
